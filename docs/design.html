Alerts & failure states

Useful signals. No constant buzzing.

01 · Arrival soon

Opt-in, one-shot. One double pulse at a fresh crossing of two minutes. Only while Metro is running.

02 · Service issue

Keep the ETA visible. A line-specific banner opens the full notice. Service vibration is off by default.

03 · Don't guess

Expired or disconnected data becomes dashes, not a frozen countdown. Pause any armed alert.

Keep Metro openNo claim of background notifications after closing the watch app. Closing cancels the one-shot arrival alert.
Fresh data before hapticsNo alert from expired data, unsupported estimates, or a threshold crossed during a connection gap.
Quiet Time stays quietVisual warnings remain. Optional haptics must respect watch Quiet Time; no sound or repeated reconnect buzzing.
A warning is not an outage detectorA failed service refresh reads “Status unavailable”. It never becomes “All normal”. Portuguese source text stays intact.

Proposed scope: favourite arrivals, service text, explicit freshness, in-app arrival alerts. No map, custom server, GPS requirement or Android service. Data attribution in the final app: UnderLX · ODbL.
